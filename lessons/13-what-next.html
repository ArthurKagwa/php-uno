Lesson 13

What next

Goal

Keep going! You now know echo, variables, maths, if/else, forms (POST and GET), switch and loops. That's enough to build real little apps.

Challenges (easy → harder)

  1. Age checker: a form asks for an age and says "child", "teen" or "adult".
  2. Tip calculator: enter a bill and a tip %, then show the tip and the total.
  3. Shillings converter: convert UGX to USD with a rate stored in a variable.
  4. Grade calculator: enter a mark out of 100 and show the grade (A, B, C, D or F).
  5. Guess the number: PHP picks rand(1, 10) and the user guesses. Say "too high" or "too low".
  6. Shopping list: store items in an array and print them with a foreach loop.

Things to learn next

  • Arrays & foreach: lists of things.
  • Functions: reusable chunks of code.
  • include: share a header and footer across pages.
  • MySQL with PDO: save data in a database. WampServer already has MySQL and phpMyAdmin at http://localhost/phpmyadmin.
  • The official manual: php.net/manual.
